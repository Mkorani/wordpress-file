== Description ==

City Government WordPress Theme. The City Town theme is a modern FSE ready block theme designed for building professional local websites. Powered by Gutenberg, it gives full creative control with pre-designed templates and flexible block patterns. The layout adapts smoothly to every device, ensuring a fully responsive experience. With clean code and cross-browser compatibility, the theme delivers fast loading and stable performance. You can easily customize colors, fonts, and sections to create a site that reflects your community, whether it is a village, town, or urban hub. This free theme suits a wide range of municipal and government projects, from local council sites to election guides and political portal pages. Users can highlight important documents, announce events, and support residents with well-structured content. Multiple layouts make it easy to publish township news, share messages from the mayor, or provide details about associations and donations. Because the theme works seamlessly with blocks, updates are simple, and creating engaging content becomes effortless. Its thoughtful design ensures a balance between simplicity and advanced options. Whether you’re building a city directory, local guide, or a townpress style website, City Town provides all the essentials. It combines speed, usability, and attractive styling so users can quickly find what they need. The flexible block system encourages creativity without requiring coding knowledge. Local politics, community activities, and council resources can be displayed in clear sections, improving navigation for visitors. With the support of block-based editing, this theme makes launching a professional portal for your township or urban community easier than ever.
